# GD Chaos Mod

Random chaos events during gameplay. Every 5 seconds, one event spawns. You have no control over what happens next.

## What's inside

**26 events**, each with its own effect:

- Gravity and size manipulation (MOON, HEAVY, MINI, BIG)
- Input and FPS chaos (HOLY INPUT LAG, SLIDE / 30 FPS, RANDOM JUMP)
- Screen effects (DISCO PARTY, EARTHQUAKE, FLASHBANG, TURN THE LIGHT ON!)
- Sound chaos (SILENCE, EARRAPE, GEOGD JUMPSCARE)
- Jumpscares and pranks (FAKE DEATH, FAKE CRASH, FAKE AD)
- Meta events (DOUBLE EVENT, CURSE, CREDITS)
- And a rare SUICIDE that just... ends your run.

## Fully customizable

Every event can be toggled on or off in **Mod Settings**. Want only jumpscares? Disable everything else. Want pain? Leave only HEAVY and SUICIDE.

## Notes

- Works on any level, in normal and practice mode.
- Does not affect level completion or stats (except SUICIDE, which is a real death).
- Safe to use in the editor playtest.
